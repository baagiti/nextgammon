Frequently asked

Do I need an account to play?

No — the App works fully offline with no sign-in. Your progress is saved on your device.

What does Run Mode include?

A one-time purchase that unlocks the full 44-stage campaign (every boss, all 45 mutation cards to collect) and removes ads from the App entirely. 1v1 Quick Match is free forever either way.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the Run Mode unlock screen and tap "Restore Purchase," signed in with the same Apple ID you purchased with.

Something's not working

Email burakakkayaa@gmail.com with a description of the issue and, if possible, your device model and iOS version — we'll get back to you.
